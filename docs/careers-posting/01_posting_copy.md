## About the role
I4I Sciences is building computational tools for biomedical discovery. We are looking for a hands-on computational scientist to lead the machine learning work, from data pipelines to model validation.

## Funding status (please read)
This position is **contingent on funding**. We are pursuing federal small-business research funding, and **no award has been made**. We are building a list of qualified candidates now. This is not a job offer, and there is no guarantee that the position will open.

## What you would do
- Build and test machine-learning models on biological data.
- Curate and clean public datasets, checking each dataset's license for commercial use.
- Set up reproducible, documented pipelines and benchmark models against agreed metrics.
- Work with domain scientists to interpret results and plan external validation.
- Report results clearly, in writing, to partners and funders.

## What we are looking for
- PhD or equivalent experience in computational biology, bioinformatics, machine learning, or a related field.
- Strong Python skills and experience with modern ML frameworks.
- Experience with biological sequence, structure, or network data.
- A habit of careful, reproducible work.

## Helpful, not required
- Experience with protein language models, graph neural networks, or interaction prediction.
- Biology or biomedical background.
- Startup or grant-funded research experience.

## Requirements
- A legal right to work in the United States.
- No conflicting obligations. Candidates must be free of any employer, institution, or agreement that would claim ownership of their work on this project.
- All inventions and work product developed for I4I Sciences are assigned to the company under a signed agreement.
